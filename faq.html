Booking & Rides

Visit our website, enter your pickup and destination details, select an available cab option and follow the booking process.

You can request cancellation through the available booking options. Please check the applicable cancellation terms before cancelling.

You can provide the correct passenger contact details and trip information while making a booking, where this option is available.

Payment & Fare

Online payments are processed through Razorpay where available. Cash payment availability depends on the booking option shown to you.

Your fare depends on the selected cab, route and applicable booking charges. Review the displayed fare before confirming your booking.

Check your payment status before trying again. If money is deducted but the booking is not confirmed, contact our support team with your transaction details.

Safety & Support

You can contact our support team through the Contact Us page, email or phone number listed on our website.

We use the information needed to provide our services. Please read our Privacy Policy to understand how your information is handled.

Contact our support team through the Contact Us page and share your booking details and a description of the issue.
