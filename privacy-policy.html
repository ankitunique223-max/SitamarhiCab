Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to Sitamarhi Cab. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Sitamarhi Cab collects, uses, stores and protects your information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name and email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Pickup and drop-off locations.
  • Booking and ride-related information.
  • Messages submitted through our contact form.
  • Technical information such as browser and device details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide cab booking services.
  • Manage customer and driver accounts.
  • Process bookings and payments.
  • Respond to customer questions and complaints.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Protect our services against fraud and misuse.

3. Firebase and Data Storage

We use Google Firebase services for authentication and database storage. Information submitted through our website may be stored securely using these services. Firebase services are subject to Google's privacy terms.

4. Payments

Our website may use Razorpay to process online payments. Payment transactions are handled by the payment provider according to its privacy and security policies. We do not store your complete card or banking credentials on our website.

5. Cookies and Advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, measure website traffic and display advertisements.

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to this website and other websites, subject to applicable Google advertising policies.

Users can manage advertising personalization through Google's advertising settings.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as Google Firebase, Google Maps, Razorpay and Google AdSense. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

10. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
