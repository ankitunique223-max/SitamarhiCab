Online Ride Booking in Sitamarhi, Bihar

SitamarhiCab is an online ride booking platform designed to help customers explore bike, auto and car booking options in Sitamarhi, Bihar. Customers can visit the booking page, enter their journey details and submit a ride request.

Available Vehicle Categories

Customers can explore different vehicle categories and select an option according to their travel needs. Actual availability depends on the service and drivers.

How to Book a Ride?

  1. Open the Book Ride page.
  2. Select your preferred vehicle.
  3. Enter pickup and destination details.
  4. Review the fare and booking information.
  5. Submit your booking request.

Fare Information

Please review the applicable fare and additional charges, if any, before confirming a ride. Final fare details should be verified on the booking page.
